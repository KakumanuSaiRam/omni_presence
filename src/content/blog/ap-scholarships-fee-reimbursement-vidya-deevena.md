College lists in counselling season are incomplete without a **fees conversation**. Andhra Pradesh runs fee-reimbursement and scholarship schemes that families know by names such as **Jagananna Vidya Deevena** and related Jagananna education schemes. Names, income ceilings, eligible courses, and disbursement rules **change**. This page will not quote rupee amounts, last year’s income limits, or a promise that your child “will get it.” Those figures belong on the current official government / Navasakam / department portal — and in a verification officer’s checklist.

## What these schemes are trying to do

In plain language, fee-reimbursement schemes pay eligible tuition (or a notified portion) to the institution for eligible students, and some schemes support other education expenses. Scholarships and reimbursements are **not the same product**. Mixing them in a WhatsApp rumour is how families assume a private-college fee is fully covered when only a notified component is.

Eligibility typically hinges on some combination of: residence and study in AP, income and family-income certificates, caste/category where the scheme is category-linked, attendance, and whether the course and college are on the eligible list. Exact combinations are in the **current** G.O. and portal FAQ. If a counselling uncle quotes a number from 2022, treat it as folklore.

## What you should actually do before web options

1. Open the official portal named in this year’s admission or social-welfare notices — not a random blog table.
2. Check whether the **college and course** you want are eligible. A seat you cannot fund is not a dream option.
3. Prepare income, caste (if applicable), Aadhaar linkage, and bank details in the formats the portal asks for. Expired income certificates are a seasonal epidemic.
4. Ask the college office how they handle reimbursement **timing**. Cash-flow gaps (family pays first, reimbursement later — or vice versa) strand students even when they are eligible on paper.

## How this changes an option list

A long EAPCET list should include campuses the family can pay **if reimbursement is delayed or rejected**. Building a list that only works if a scheme pays on time is how reporting week becomes a crisis. Category and income also affect reservation — do not confuse a reservation claim with a reimbursement claim; both need documents, and they are different officers.

## Scams and shortcuts

Nobody can “guarantee” reimbursement for a fee. Do not pay a middleman to “speed up” a government portal. Do not share OTPs. If a college asks you to sign papers that contradict the portal, pause and ask the counselling desk to read them.
