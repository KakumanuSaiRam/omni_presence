Most BiPC students prepare for NEET with everything they have — and prepare for nothing else. The EAPCET pharmacy stream is the second ace they forget they're holding.

## The stream most BiPC students ignore

AP EAPCET has a dedicated **agriculture & pharmacy stream** testing Botany, Zoology, Physics and Chemistry — the BiPC student's own subjects. A student already deep in NEET preparation can write this stream with minimal extra effort, and it opens B.Pharmacy, Pharm.D, agricultural and allied seats across Andhra Pradesh.

## Why pharmacy deserves respect

- **Pharm.D and B.Pharmacy careers** span hospital pharmacy, clinical research, regulatory affairs and the pharma industry — one of India's strongest export sectors.
- **Higher studies**: GPAT to M.Pharm, or global routes into clinical research.
- **Realistic cutoffs**: good pharmacy colleges admit at ranks far gentler than MBBS cutoffs, making it a strong plan-B that many families would be happy with as plan-A.

## The double-counselling season

A BiPC student with both a NEET score and an EAPCET pharmacy rank navigates **two counselling calendars** — NEET state-quota rounds and EAPCET web options — whose deadlines interleave. Families managing this alone routinely lock a pharmacy seat too early or hold out too long for a medical round that was never realistic.
