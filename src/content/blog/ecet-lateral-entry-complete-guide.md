A polytechnic diploma is not a dead end. **AP ECET** is the official lateral-entry exam that places diploma holders into the **second year of B.Tech**. Families who chose POLYCET after tenth should treat ECET as part of the original plan, not a surprise three years later. This is the path we map at the graduate counselling desk in Guntur.

## Who writes ECET, and what it buys

ECET is for students who already hold (or are completing) a qualifying diploma in a relevant branch, and for some B.Sc. streams under the notified rules of that year. A successful rank plus counselling can skip first-year B.Tech and join a college through the lateral-entry quota. That is why government polytechnic + ECET is often the **lowest-cost engineering runway** in Andhra Pradesh — if the diploma years are taken seriously.

Eligibility, paper pattern and the exact diploma-to-branch mapping are notified each season. Do not rely on a senior’s memory from two years ago. Read the current notification, then sit with someone who has filled ECET web options before.

## What to study while you are still in diploma

ECET tests diploma-level engineering plus a mathematics/physics/chemistry core, depending on the paper you are allotted. Students who treat diploma internals as “just pass” arrive at ECET with weak fundamentals. The practical habit that helps: keep first-year and second-year diploma notes, rework numericals, and write previous ECET papers under time in the final diploma year.

If you are still in Class 10 and weighing POLYCET, this is the honest pitch: the diploma is worthwhile when you **intend** ECET (or a job at 18). It is a poor default if the family actually wanted a four-year campus from day one and will resent the lateral-entry process.

## Counselling is a second web-options season

ECET has its own certificate verification, option entry and allotment rounds. Diploma memos, study certificates and category papers must be current. Branch choice is constrained by what your diploma qualifies you for — a computer diploma does not magically open every engineering seat. Build a long ordered list: preferred colleges first, then realistic, then safety, the same discipline as EAPCET.

Common failures: filling too few options, ignoring colleges outside the home district, and missing a reporting date because a job or diploma project was “almost done”.

## Diploma job vs B.Tech — decide with numbers, not prestige

Some diploma holders should take a job or apprenticeship and write ECET later; some should go straight to B.Tech. The decision hangs on family budget, the student’s stamina for two more years of academics, and whether the lateral-entry college-branch pair on offer is actually better than working. There is no universal right answer. There is a wrong one: drifting for a year with neither a job plan nor an ECET application.
