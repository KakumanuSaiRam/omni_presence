Guntur and coastal-Andhra families often write **TS EAMCET** as a second shot at Hyderabad-region colleges. Writing the exam is the easy half. Filling Telangana web options as a non-local candidate is where lists go wrong. This guide is the counselling-desk version we walk through at Siva Tuitions & Coachings, Guntur.

## Confirm you are actually eligible to sit in that seat

TS EAMCET counselling is not a copy of AP EAPCET. Telangana applies its own **local / non-local** and reservation rules. Many Andhra students assume a strong rank unlocks the same colleges a Hyderabad student sees. It does not. Before you open the portal, establish: your TS rank (and category rank if shown), whether you count as non-local, and which reservation benefits — if any — actually apply to you. If a parent cannot answer those three points in one sentence, the list is not ready.

## Build two lists, not one

Keep an **AP list** and a **TS list** on paper. Do not merge them in your head. A college that looks “safe” on AP EAPCET may be unreachable on TS EAMCET for a non-local rank, and the reverse is also true. Our desk typically prepares a long ordered TS list — dream options first, then realistic, then genuine safety — the same discipline as AP, with Telangana-specific college codes.

Never type the list live into the portal on day one. Portal sessions time out; paper does not.

## Order by preference, then check the calendar clash

The allotment walk is top-to-bottom: you get the first available seat on your ordered list. Putting an ambitious Hyderabad branch first does not cost you a safer option lower down. What *does* cost seats is a **calendar clash** — AP and TS rounds sometimes overlap in the same week. Decide in advance which state’s self-reporting you will honour if both allot, and which you will slide or reject. Families who treat this as a last-minute WhatsApp debate lose both seats.

## Documents Telangana will actually ask for

Expect SSC and Intermediate memos, TS EAMCET hall ticket and rank card, study certificates, and category/income papers if you are claiming them. Non-local Andhra students should keep extra copies of study and residence proof ready; a missing page stalls verification even when the rank is good. Do not reuse an expired income certificate from last year’s AP counselling.

## Mistakes we see every season

- Copying a Hyderabad classmate’s list (different local status = different reality)
- Filling 15 options and hoping rank will “do the rest”
- Ignoring pharmacy or core-engineering safety options
- Missing a TS reporting deadline because AP counselling was on the same day
- Treating a Hyderabad management-quota enquiry as a counselling plan
