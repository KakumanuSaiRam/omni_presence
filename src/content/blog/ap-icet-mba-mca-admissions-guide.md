Engineering counselling gets the noise. **AP ICET** — the Integrated Common Entrance Test for **MBA and MCA** seats in Andhra Pradesh — is quieter, and families therefore improvise. Improvisation is how graduates with decent ranks land colleges they would not have chosen with a written list. This is the graduate-desk walkthrough we use in Guntur.

## What ICET is for

ICET is the state entrance used for MBA and MCA admissions in participating universities and affiliated colleges. A bachelor’s degree with the notified minimum marks (and mathematics at the required level for MCA, where the notification says so) is the usual gate. Exact eligibility, paper pattern and qualifying marks are published each year — treat last year’s brochure as orientation, not law.

## Rank is not a college name

An ICET rank without a college-and-specialisation list is a rumour. Before option entry, write down:

- MBA vs MCA (do not “see how the rank goes” on the portal)
- Location constraints (Guntur / Vijayawada / Hyderabad-adjacent / willing to relocate)
- Budget band the family can actually pay after any fee-reimbursement eligibility is checked — not hoped
- Specialisation interest (finance, marketing, HR, systems, analytics) versus “any seat”

University campuses, autonomous colleges and private affiliates are not interchangeable. Ask about faculty stability, internships, and whether the campus still runs the specialisation you want. Placement banners are marketing. Attendance rules and fee circulars are facts.

## Web options still punish short lists

ICET counselling uses certificate verification and web options. Filling a handful of familiar college names is the graduate version of the EAPCET mistake we see every summer. Prepare a long ordered list: preference first, then realistic, then safety. Category, local area and gender (where applicable) change what “realistic” means. Do not copy a classmate’s MBA list onto an MCA form.

## Documents and timelines

Keep degree memos (or provisional), Intermediate and SSC, ICET hall ticket and rank card, study certificates, and caste/income papers if claimed. MCA hopefuls should confirm mathematics requirements in writing from the notification, not from a coaching-centre rumour. Missed verification slots are not recovered by a strong rank.

If you are a final-year student, do not wait for the convocation photo to start the file. Provisional certificates and backlog rules are where last-week panics live.

## Guntur graduates who commute vs relocate

Many ICET ranks from Guntur, Tenali and Vijayawada are used on colleges in the same belt so the student can stay home. That is a valid constraint — write it at the top of the list. It is not valid to hide it and then reject every Hyderabad or Visakhapatnam allotment in reporting week. If the family will not relocate, do not put those campuses in the dream band “for rank’s sake.” If they will relocate for a university campus, say so before option entry so the list is not a fiction.

## After allotment

Self-report, fee payment and sliding decisions have deadlines. Rejecting a seat because a private “management” conversation is “almost confirmed” is how students finish September with neither.
