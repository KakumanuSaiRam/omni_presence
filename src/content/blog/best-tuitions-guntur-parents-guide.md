"Best tuition near me" returns a wall of ads. Here is a calmer way to decide, from a centre that has watched Guntur students grow from Class 1 to college seats.

## Match the centre to the board first

CBSE, ICSE and AP State syllabus differ in depth, language and exam pattern. A centre that teaches all three should run **separate batches** for them — ask to see the timetable. Mixed-board batches quietly shortchange one group.

## Small batches, visible notebooks

The test of a tuition centre is whether the tutor knows each child's weak chapters by name. That requires small batches and checked notebooks. Visit unannounced and look at what students are actually writing.

## A feedback loop with parents

You should hear about a slipping test score within the week — not at the year-end result. Ask how the centre communicates: monthly reports, WhatsApp updates, or silence.

## Continuity matters more than parents expect

A child who changes tuition centres every year loses months to re-adjustment. Centres that teach the full arc — school classes, then Intermediate, then entrance coaching — carry knowledge of your child forward. This continuity is central to how **Siva Tuitions & Coachings** works: students who join in school classes move into our Intermediate tuitions and EAPCET/NEET/POLYCET batches with teachers who already know them.

## Questions to ask on your first visit

1. How many students per batch, in my child's class and board?
2. Who exactly teaches, and what are their qualifications?
3. How often are tests held, and can I see a sample report?
4. What happens when a child falls behind?
5. What do fees include — materials, tests, extra doubt sessions?
