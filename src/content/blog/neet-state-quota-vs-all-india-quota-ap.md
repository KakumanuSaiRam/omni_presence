NEET UG is one exam and **two (sometimes more) counselling tracks**. Families in Guntur who only watch the AP medical counselling portal miss All India rounds; families who only watch MCC miss state deadlines. This is the split we explain at every NEET counselling session.

## The two main tracks

- **All India Quota (AIQ)** — a share of seats in government medical and dental colleges nationwide, counselled through the designated national (MCC) process, with its own registration, choice filling and rounds.
- **Andhra Pradesh state quota** — seats in AP government and private medical/dental colleges counselled through the state portal, with AP’s reservation, local-area and documentation rules.

There can also be deemed-university, central-university and private management routes with **separate** registrations. “We registered somewhere” is not a plan. Write down every portal the student’s score actually qualifies them to enter, with that portal’s closing date.

## A rank means different things on each track

The same NEET score can look hopeful on one list and hopeless on another. Category, state of eligibility, and whether the student is local to AU/SVU (for AP rules) change the picture. Do not convert an All India closing rank you saw on YouTube into an AP government-college promise. Do not assume a strong state rank automatically wins an AIQ seat in another state.

Our desk maps score → realistic college bands **per track**, then decides which rounds to play. That mapping uses the latest official round data, not a printed chart from two years ago.

## Documents and category claims must match everywhere

Caste, EWS, disability and income certificates that work on one portal can fail on another if the format, issuing authority or validity date differs. Get the current checklists for MCC and for AP counselling and prepare **both** files. A last-week income certificate is a seasonal tradition we would like to retire.

## Parallel deadlines are the real risk

AIQ and state rounds overlap. A student who does not upgrade, who forgets to report, or who lets an AIQ seat lapse while waiting for a state dream college can lose **both**. Decide freeze/upgrade rules in writing, the same way JoSAA families decide freeze/float.

If the NEET score is below a realistic MBBS band, plan the **fallback in the same week**: BDS/BAMS rounds where eligible, B.Pharmacy via EAPCET, allied health, or a structured repeat year. Waiting until “medical counselling is fully over” to look at pharmacy is how BiPC students lose EAPCET options too.

## One checklist, two portals

Bring rank card, hall ticket, class 10 and 12 memos, study certificates, and every reservation paper you intend to use. We tick them against **both** counselling brochures, not against memory.
