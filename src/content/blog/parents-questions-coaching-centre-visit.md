A website — including this one — is not a visit. If you are choosing EAPCET, NEET, POLYCET or Intermediate tuitions in Guntur, **walk in with a list**. Centres are used to parents who ask only about fees and last year’s toppers. Ask operational questions. Then listen for vagueness.

## Batch and teacher

- How many students are in the batch my child would join — not the “demo” batch?
- Who teaches each subject **this year**? Can I meet them today?
- If a famous name is on the flex, how many hours do they actually teach this batch?

Small batches are not a slogan; they are a number you can count in a room. At Siva Tuitions & Coachings we keep batches small so doubts get asked. Apply the same test to us: sit in, count.

## Tests and board balance

- Show me last month’s test calendar and one marked script (with permission).
- How do you handle IPE versus EAPCET/NEET — integrated, or “entrance only”?
- What happens in the first week if my child joins late?

No calendar usually means no rhythm. “We complete syllabus” without mocks is a lecture shop.

## After the rank — the question most parents skip

- Do you help with **certificate verification and web options**, or does the relationship end on results day?
- Is counselling included, separately charged, or only for “rankers”?
- Can students from outside Guntur get phone/WhatsApp option-list reviews?

Siva Tuitions & Coachings runs a dedicated admission-counselling desk for AP EAPCET, TS EAMCET, NEET and POLYCET. That is our differentiator; it is also a claim you should verify by asking what last season’s process looked like — documents, list length, who sat with the family.

## Fees, refunds, and pressure

- What is included (material, mocks, counselling) and what is extra?
- What is the refund or batch-change rule in writing?
- Are you being asked to pay two years on the spot before a trial week?

We will not invent a fee table on this page; amounts change and should come from the office. Distrust any centre that **guarantees** a rank or a college.

## Materials, app, and “online extras”

Ask whether printed material is included, whether an app is required, and who answers doubts after 8 p.m. An Android app is a supplement — Siva Tuitions has one on Google Play — not a substitute for a teacher who is in the room. If the selling point is only recorded videos, you are buying a library card. If the selling point is only “personal attention” with no test file, you are buying a story.

## Safety and access

- Lighting, washrooms, waiting space for a parent
- How you are told if a class is cancelled
- Phone policy during tests
