Guntur’s Intermediate market is loud: ranks on flex banners, “corporate” branding, and pressure to decide in a week after SSC. A campus is where your child will spend two years that feed **IPE marks and EAPCET/NEET**. Choose it like a workplace inspection, not like a wedding venue.

## Start with logistics, not the hoarding

Write the commute in minutes at 7:30 a.m., not at noon. A 90-minute one-way trip plus tuitions is how Inter students lose sleep and then “don’t like MPC.” If the honest commute is brutal, consider a closer campus or a documented hostel plan — not optimism.

Confirm the **group** the campus actually teaches well. Some junior colleges are MPC-heavy in culture; BiPC students become an afterthought. Ask to see a recent internal test paper in *your* group, not a brochure collage.

## Faculty you can meet, tests you can see

Ask who teaches first-year Mathematics or Zoology **this year**, and whether you can meet them. Star names who visit twice a semester are advertising. Then ask for last month’s test calendar: weekly tests, slip tests, and whether answer sheets come back with comments. A college that cannot show a testing rhythm will not magically produce one in January.

If you also plan external tuitions (many Guntur families do), ask how the college treats that — some punish it, some ignore it, some coordinate. Conflict between college homework and tuition homework is a standard stressor. Name it before you pay a fee.

## Board result vs entrance — refuse the false war

A college that says “forget IPE, only EAPCET” is selling a trade-off the public exam will not honour. A college that says “only IPE, entrance is extra” dumps the entrance onto a tuition centre and then blames it. The workable model is **integrated**: board syllabus taught thoroughly, MCQ speed trained on a clock. That can happen inside a college, in a tuition centre, or in both — but someone must own the weekly MCQ test.

## Red flags

- Refusing a campus walkthrough of actual classrooms
- No sample test paper
- Pressure to pay the full two-year fee before you compare two campuses
- “Guaranteed rank” language (no honest centre should say this)
- Hostel stories you cannot verify with a current parent
