Our counselling desk in Guntur reviews hundreds of option lists every season. The same seven mistakes appear every single year.

## 1. Filling too few options

The algorithm can only give you a seat from the list you typed. Students with 15 options routinely go seatless while lower-ranked students with 120 options get placed. There is no penalty for long lists. Fill generously.

## 2. Copying a friend's list

Your friend's category, local area, and budget are not yours. An OC non-local list is useless to a BC-B local candidate with the same rank. Every list must be built from the student's own certificates.

## 3. Ordering by predicted cutoff instead of preference

Students "save" top options thinking they won't get them. The algorithm reads top to bottom — putting a dream option first costs nothing. Order by desire, not by fear.

## 4. Ignoring the second phase

Families treat first-phase allotment as final. Cutoffs move in later phases as students slide and leave. Understand the sliding rules before accepting or rejecting anything.

## 5. Certificate problems discovered late

Expired income certificates, mismatched name spellings between memos, missing study certificates — each of these has derailed real admissions. Verify the document checklist a month early.

## 6. BiPC students skipping pharmacy options

BiPC students fixated only on MBBS often skip EAPCET's pharmacy and agriculture stream entirely — then have nothing when NEET counselling disappoints. Fill both tracks in parallel.

## 7. Waiting for spot admissions

Spot admission is the leftovers table, not a strategy. By that round, the seats worth wanting are gone.
