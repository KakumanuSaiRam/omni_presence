A JEE Main (or Advanced) rank is not a seat. **JoSAA** is the joint seat-allocation process for IITs, NITs, IIITs and GFTIs. Andhra families usually run JoSAA **in parallel with AP EAPCET**, which is how both a good NIT option and a good JNTU option get dropped in the same week. This is the round-by-round picture we use at the graduate desk in Guntur.

## Choice filling is the whole game

You submit an ordered list of institute-and-programme pairs. The algorithm walks that list and offers the best seat you can get with your rank, category and home-state / other-state rules. There is no bonus for “being reasonable” at the top. Put genuine first preferences first. A short list is the same disaster it is in EAPCET: the algorithm cannot allot a programme you never listed.

Write the list on paper with the student, not the uncle who “knows NITs”. Home State quota at an NIT is not the same as Other State. AP students targeting NIT Warangal, NIT Trichy or similar must know which quota they sit in.

## Freeze, float, slide — pick in writing before the clock

After an allotment you typically choose:

- **Freeze** — you take this seat; you are done sliding.
- **Float** — you keep this seat unless a *better* choice on your list opens later.
- **Slide** — you stay in the same institute but will take a better programme there if it opens.

The words are easy. The family argument is not. Decide the rule *before* Round 1: for example, “we freeze any IIT”, or “we float until NIT X CSE is gone, then freeze ECE there”. Changing the rule under WhatsApp pressure mid-round is how people reject a seat they later want.

## Document verification is not optional

JoSAA will not wait because an income or category certificate is “being renewed”. Keep JEE documents, class 12 marks, category papers and photo ID ready in the format the current brochure asks for. A mismatch between category claimed in JEE and category certificate is a classic stall.

## CSAB is not a consolation prize you ignore

After JoSAA, **CSAB** special rounds often open leftover NIT/IIIT/GFTI seats. Some AP students who freeze an EAPCET college too early, or who skip CSAB because “JoSAA is over”, leave seats on the table. Track both calendars. If you accept an AP EAPCET seat, know the withdrawal and fee rules *before* you also hold a JoSAA seat — dual reporting without a plan creates fee and cancellation messes.

## Run JoSAA and EAPCET as one decision tree

Every round, write one sentence: “If JoSAA offers X and EAPCET offers Y, we take ___.” If you cannot finish that sentence, you are not ready to freeze anything.
