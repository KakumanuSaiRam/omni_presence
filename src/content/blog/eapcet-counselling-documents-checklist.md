Certificate verification is the least glamorous and most failure-prone step of counselling. Use this checklist a month before your slot, not the night before.

## The core documents

1. **EAPCET rank card and hall ticket**
2. **SSC (10th) marks memo** — the name spelling here is your canonical name; every other document must match it
3. **Intermediate marks memo and transfer certificate**
4. **Study certificates** — classes 6 to 12, from each school/college attended; these establish your **local status** (AU region for Guntur students)
5. **Caste certificate** (BC/SC/ST) — issued by MeeSeva, in the student's name
6. **Income certificate** — must be **recent**; expired income certificates are the single most common verification failure, and they cost fee-reimbursement eligibility
7. **Aadhaar card**
8. **EWS certificate** where applicable (OC candidates claiming EWS)
9. **Special category proofs** — NCC, sports, CAP, PH certificates if claimed

## The mistakes that stall allotments

- **Name mismatches** between SSC memo and caste certificate ("Y. Murali" vs "Yadlapalli Murali") — fix via the issuing office before counselling.
- **Missing a study certificate** for one year — schools take days to reissue; start early.
- **Income certificate in the wrong parent's name** or older than the validity window.
- **Assuming private candidates don't need study certificates** — residence certificates replace them, with their own processing time.

## Keep three sets

One original set, two photocopy sets, and clear phone scans of everything in a single folder. Counselling centres and colleges each keep copies at different steps.
