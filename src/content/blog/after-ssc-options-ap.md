The weeks after SSC results are when families make their most consequential education decision — usually with the least information. Here is the full map.

## Path 1: Intermediate

The default path, but "Intermediate" hides four very different groups:

- **MPC** — for engineering (EAPCET/JEE) and defence-academy routes. Choose only if Maths is a genuine strength.
- **BiPC** — for medicine (NEET), pharmacy, agriculture and life sciences. Heavier memorisation load; strong career breadth beyond MBBS.
- **MEC/CEC** — for commerce, CA/CMA, law and civil-services foundations. Wrongly treated as a "backup"; for the right student it is the fastest professional route.

The error we counsel against most: choosing MPC by social default when the child's aptitude clearly points elsewhere.

## Path 2: Polytechnic (POLYCET)

A three-year diploma after tenth, then a job at 18 or **ECET lateral entry** directly into B.Tech second year. Lowest-cost route to engineering; includes dedicated government polytechnics for women. Best for hands-on learners and budget-conscious families. See our full POLYCET guide for details.

## Path 3: ITI

Two-year skill trades with genuine employment demand — electrician, fitter, welder, COPA. Shortest route to earning; apprenticeship routes into public-sector units are underrated.

## How to actually decide

Three questions cut through most confusion:

1. **Aptitude** — what do the child's marks and interest consistently say, subject by subject?
2. **Goal** — is there a concrete target (doctor, engineer, government job), or is flexibility more valuable?
3. **Budget & runway** — can the family fund a 6-year runway (Inter + degree), or is a 3-year route wiser?
