A B.Sc. is not an unfinished B.Tech. It is a degree with **several postgraduate and job gates**, and families often only hear “do M.Sc.” **AP PG-CET** (and related university PG admissions) is one gate. ICET (MBA/MCA), teaching pathways, and government-exam prep are others. The graduate counselling desk in Guntur exists because a final-year B.Sc. student is asked to choose in a month with almost no map.

## PG-CET and M.Sc. / related PG

State PG common tests and university-specific admissions place students into M.Sc. and other notified PG programmes. Eligibility is subject-specific: a chemistry B.Sc. does not automatically open every life-science PG. Read the **current** PG-CET / university notification for subjects, qualifying marks and counselling.

Choose a PG if the student wants deeper subject work, lab or teaching routes that expect a master’s, or a specific university department they can name. Do not choose M.Sc. only to postpone a job decision for two years — that is how families fund a degree nobody wanted.

Counselling still needs documents, web options or offline reporting (as that year specifies), and a list longer than three famous campuses.

## When ICET is the better fork

If the student wants **MBA or MCA**, AP ICET is the usual state entrance, not PG-CET. MCA typically cares about mathematics background as notified that year. MBA cares about whether the student will actually do business coursework. ## Jobs and exams without a PG

Some B.Sc. graduates should work, intern, or prepare for notified government recruitments (roles change; check current notifications). Teaching may require B.Ed. or other qualifications depending on the school type — verify, do not assume a B.Sc. alone is enough. Lab technician, data, and private-sector science jobs exist; they require applications, not a counselling round.

The honest session asks: **earn now, PG now, or professional master’s (MBA/MCA) now?** Mixing all three as “we’ll see” produces a gap year by accident.

## Subject-wise forks we see most

A B.Sc. (Mathematics) student is often closer to MCA, data roles, or teaching maths than to an M.Sc. they do not want. A B.Sc. (Life Sciences) student may be looking at M.Sc., allied-health PG, or a job — not an accidental MBA. A B.Sc. (Computers) student should not skip MCA eligibility rules and then discover them at ICET verification. Bring the exact degree title on the memo; “B.Sc.” is not a subject.

## Documents and timing

Keep semester memos, consolidated marks, transfer and study certificates, and category/income papers if you will claim them. Final-year students should track application windows before convocation photos. Backlogs are a portal problem, not a motivational one.
