Guntur has no shortage of coaching centres. Here is the checklist our own students' parents found most useful when comparing options — apply it to us too.

## 1. Batch size beats brand size

A famous name with 120 students per hall cannot notice that your child is stuck on rotational dynamics. Ask directly: how many students sit in one batch? Under 30 means doubts actually get asked. At Siva Tuitions & Coachings we keep batches small for exactly this reason.

## 2. Who actually teaches?

Big banners often advertise star faculty who teach only the "elite" batch. Ask who will teach *your* batch, and meet them. At our Pattabhipuram centre, director Yadlapalli Naga Murali Krishna (M.B.A, M.Tech CSE, M.Sc, MCA) is personally involved in the classroom, not just the brochure.

## 3. Test rhythm and analysis

EAPCET rewards speed and accuracy under a clock. Weekly part-tests plus regular full-length grand tests — with per-question analysis afterwards — matter more than extra lecture hours. Ask any centre to show you last month's test calendar.

## 4. Board–entrance balance

Intermediate (IPE) marks still matter for your future applications, and the EAPCET syllabus is the Intermediate syllabus. Coaching that dismisses board exams pushes students into a false trade-off. Integrated preparation is the honest approach.

## 5. What happens after the rank?

This is the differentiator almost nobody checks. A rank without guided counselling can still end in a poor seat. Ask: does the centre help with certificate verification and web options, or does its responsibility end on results day?
