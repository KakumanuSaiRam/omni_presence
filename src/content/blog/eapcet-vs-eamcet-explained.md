Parents still say "EAMCET"; portals now say "EAPCET". Here is the short version of what changed and why it barely matters for preparation.

## The name change

Andhra Pradesh renamed its exam **AP EAPCET** — Engineering, Agriculture and Pharmacy Common Entrance Test — reflecting that the exam covers more than engineering. Telangana continues with **TS EAMCET** for its own admissions. Both descend from the same original EAMCET, and colloquially the names are interchangeable.

## Who takes which?

- **AP EAPCET**: for seats in Andhra Pradesh colleges (JNTU-K, AU, SVU regions and private colleges in AP).
- **TS EAMCET**: for seats in Telangana colleges (JNTU-H, OU, KU regions).
- Students can write **both** — many Guntur students do, since Hyderabad's college ecosystem is a real option.

## Syllabus overlap

Both exams test the Intermediate syllabus — Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry for MPC; Botany, Zoology, Physics, Chemistry for BiPC. The overlap is nearly complete, so one well-run preparation covers both exams. The differences are administrative: separate applications, separate hall tickets, separate counselling portals with different local-area rules.

## Counselling is where they truly diverge

Writing both exams is easy; navigating **two counselling processes** is where families struggle. AP and Telangana have different certificate requirements, different local/non-local classifications, and different web-options timelines that sometimes overlap in the same week.
