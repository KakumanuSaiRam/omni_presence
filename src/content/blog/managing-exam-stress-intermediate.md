Intermediate in Andhra Pradesh stacks **public exams and entrance exams** in the same two years. Stress is not a character flaw. Unmanaged stress does show up as ranks and as health. This is not a meditation brand page. It is what we tell families in Guntur when a student freezes in mocks.

## What exam stress often looks like (so you stop calling it laziness)

- Sleep that starts at 1 a.m. “because Physics”
- Stomach aches on test days
- Perfect notes, unfinished papers
- Irritation at home that is actually fear of a weekly rank list
- A sudden drop after one bad grand test

If this lasts weeks, treat it as a timetable and health problem, not as a motivation speech.

## Habits that reduce panic more than posters do

**Sleep a set window.** Entrance prep that steals sleep is fake prep. Shift hard subjects earlier in the day; put light revision near sleep.

**Write timed papers in the same room conditions you can control.** Panic often appears when the first timed paper is the real one. Weekly mocks at Siva Tuitions exist partly to make the clock boring.

**Separate “I don’t know this chapter” from “I went blank.”** The first needs teaching. The second needs slower starts: easy questions first, a planned skip rule, water, and a parent who does not replay the blank at dinner.

**One adult who is not scoring the child.** A parent can own food and logistics. A tutor can own error logs. If both become rank commentators, the student has nowhere to put fear.

## What parents should stop doing in the last 60 days

- Comparing cousin percentiles at breakfast
- Adding a fourth tuition “just in case”
- Treating every mock as a career verdict
- Debating MPC vs BiPC again in Inter second year

Ask instead: “Did you sleep? Did you log errors? Do you need a doubt slot?” That is support. Rank lectures are noise.

## Mock day protocol

On grand-test days: normal breakfast, reach early, no new chapter in the auto. After the paper, wait twenty minutes before discussing marks. Parents: do not open the rank list in the car. Students: write three sentences in the error log before you look at a phone. The ritual sounds small. It is how a bad mock stays a data point instead of a family fight.

## When to get medical or counselling help

If there is self-harm talk, panic attacks, or a collapse in eating and school attendance, you need a **doctor or qualified counsellor**, not another crash course. Coaching centres are not hospitals. We will say that in the room.

## Keep the academic loop kind and strict

Kind: no humiliation in class for a wrong answer. Strict: the mock still happens. Students recover faster in a centre that does both. Our Pattabhipuram batches are small enough that a freeze is visible — which is the point of a small batch.
