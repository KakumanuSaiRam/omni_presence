## Hook

ICET is not “some MBA test.” It is the AP portal that allots MBA and MCA seats. A rank without an option list still strands you.

## Talking points

1. After ICET, registration, certificates, web options, allotment — same discipline as EAPCET.
2. MBA versus MCA: read this year’s eligibility. MCA often needs a maths or CS background.
3. CAT/MAT are a different campus set. Many graduates sit both and use ICET as the seat they can actually join.
4. Check whether the college is on the current ICET list and what they charge if reimbursement is late.
5. Same desk also does ECET, PG-CET and JoSAA. Bring the degree memo, not only the rank card.
