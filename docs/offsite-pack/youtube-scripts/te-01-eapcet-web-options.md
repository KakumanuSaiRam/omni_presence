## Description (two paragraphs + NAP + site)

AP EAPCET web options are an ordered list of college-and-branch pairs, not a form you merely complete. This short Telugu briefing covers the mistakes that leave students seatless after a decent rank: too few options, wrong category cutoffs, and certificate name mismatches at verification.

Director Yadlapalli Naga Murali Krishna (M.B.A, M.Tech CSE, M.Sc, MCA) records this from the counselling desk at Siva Tuitions & Coachings. Coaching and option-list review happen at one classroom only.
