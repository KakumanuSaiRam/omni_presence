## Talking points

1. 10th lo Maths, Science marks *and* morning study habit chudandi.
2. MPC ante engineering / physical sciences — EAPCET MPC, optional JEE.
3. BiPC ante medical, pharmacy, agriculture — NEET plus EAPCET BiPC.
4. First year tarvata group marchadam kashtam. Sticky choice.
5. “Doctor kavali” anesari Biology kastam aithe BiPC vaddu. “CSE package” kosam Maths wall aithe MPC vaddu.
6. MEC/CEC kuda unnai. MPC only safe ani force cheyakandi.
